# Guide 9.5 — Edits made while playtesting

**This isn't part of the original planned curriculum.** Everything below was
requested while playtesting Guide 8 (or right after finishing Guide 9) —
tuning changes and small new features that came up from actually playing the
game, not pre-planned content. Originally written up as separate Guides 10,

## From Guide 10 — King Damage & the Cyclops telegraph attack

Two additions: a per-monster **King Damage** value, and the **Cyclops**'s
telegraphed area attack (wind-up box → slam), including its later pause/ramp
refinement.

**King Damage** is a plain field on every monster definition (next to Player
Damage / Structure Damage) — always used for hits on the King, never a
fallback to Player Damage, never stacked with anything. Set it equal to
Player Damage by hand if you want a monster to hit the King the same as it
hits the player. (This started as a checkbox-gated "Unique King Damage"
toggle; the toggle was removed later — see the Guide 12 section below —
since the field being its own always-used value made the toggle redundant.)

**Cyclops telegraphed area attack:** it winds up before it slams — a gray
box appears over its target's locked position and fills in over Telegraph
Time, then a black box flashes and damages everything caught inside it.
Setup:

1. `Monster` prefab → **Add Component → Telegraphed Area Attack** → wire
   **Box Sprite** to the `Square` sprite from `Assets/Sprites`.
2. On `Assets/Monsters/Cyclops`, under **Special: Cyclops — telegraphed
   area attack**:
   - **Uses Telegraphed Area Attack** — check it.
   - **Attack Box Size** — `2, 2`.
   - **Telegraph Time** — `1.5` (wind-up seconds).
   - **Telegraph Base Color** / **Telegraph Fill Color** — transparent gray
     / darker transparent gray (the wind-up boxes).
   - **Slam Color** — transparent black (the impact flash).
   - **Slam Flash Seconds** — `0.2`.
   - **Pauses During Telegraph** — checkbox, on by default. Eases the
     Cyclops to a stop as the wind-up begins, and back up to speed once
     cooldown starts. Uncheck it to have the Cyclops keep walking normally
     through the whole attack (the box still locks and lands on schedule).
   - **Telegraph Stop Duration** — seconds to decelerate to a stop. `0` =
     instant. Default `0.2`.
   - **Telegraph Resume Duration** — seconds to accelerate back to full
     speed after the slam. `0` = instant. Default `0.3`.
   - Make sure **Attack Range** (~`1.5`–`1.8`) and **Attack Interval**
     (`2.5`, now the cooldown between slams) are set.

The box is **locked** to where the target stood when the wind-up started —
step out during the wind-up and the slam misses. Stop/Resume Duration are
deliberately separate timers from Telegraph Time and Attack Interval, so
tuning the movement feel never distorts the dodge window or attack cadence.

### Checklist
- [ ] King Damage is its own always-used value
- [ ] Cyclops winds up a filling gray box, then a black slam; dodging out during wind-up avoids it
- [ ] Pauses During Telegraph checked: Cyclops eases to a stop, then eases back up to speed
- [ ] Pauses During Telegraph unchecked: Cyclops keeps walking the whole attack

## From Guide 11 — Targeting tuning, DPS readout & skeleton fix

A batch of quality-of-life and targeting refinements.

**Estimated DPS readout (automatic):** every monster definition's Inspector
shows a blue info box at the top — damage-per-second vs Player, King,
Structure, and Praise Tower, computed from the damage fields ÷ the attack
cycle (telegraph wind-up included for the Cyclops). Updates live as you edit
fields below it.

**Reorganized inspector (automatic):** fields are grouped under clear
headers — Identity, Movement & health, Damage dealt, Attack timing & reach,
Targeting — player vs King, Targeting — structures, Economy & weight, then
the per-monster specials (Goblin / Skeleton / Cyclops).

**Skeleton bone-pile fix (automatic):** while a Skeleton is a bone pile, its
health bar is now fully hidden (no lingering sliver), and it's completely
frozen — physics off, so it can't creep or be shoved, and towers/your sword
ignore it entirely until revival.

**New targeting knobs**, under **Targeting — player vs King**:

- **King Priority Beats Structures** (checkbox) — normally structure-
  priority outranks the King. Check this and, whenever the King is within
  **King Priority Range**, the King wins over nearby structures too.
- **Keep Target Within Range** (tiles, `0` = off) — if a monster is within
  this many tiles of the King/structure it's targeting, it ignores the
  player entirely, even while being attacked. Fixes "the player kept
  pulling monsters off the King even with a clear path." Try `1`.

### Checklist
- [ ] DPS box shows at the top of each monster definition
- [ ] Skeleton bone pile hides its health bar and doesn't move at all
- [ ] King Priority Beats Structures makes a close monster pick the King over a tower
- [ ] Keep Target Within Range stops the player peeling a monster off a point-blank King

## From Guide 12 — Structure targeting reworked

A cleanup pass on monster targeting/damage, reworking the "detour to a
structure vs. head to the King" logic so a monster can no longer loop
around the outside of the map forever finding "the nearest structure" and
never actually reaching the King.

- **Use Unique King Damage checkbox removed.** Covered in the Guide 10
  section above — King Damage is just its own always-used field now.
- **Prioritizes Structures checkbox removed.** It was redundant: Structure
  Priority Range and Structure Interest Range (below) already mean "off" at
  `0`, the same convention every other range field here uses. That field
  being nonzero is what turns structure-priority on now, no separate
  toggle needed.
- **Structure Far King Ratio / Structure Notice Radius replaced by
  Structure Interest Range** (tiles, `0` = off). Within this range, a
  monster prefers a structure over trekking to the King — still only
  competes with heading to the King, never with actively chasing the
  player. **Guard:** the candidate structure must also be closer to the
  King than the monster currently is — this makes it geometrically
  impossible for a monster to get lured sideways or backward into looping
  around the map's perimeter, since every detour is now guaranteed to be a
  step toward the King.
- **New: Structure Near King Range** (tiles, `0` = off) — if a candidate
  structure from either Structure Priority Range or Structure Interest
  Range is within this many tiles of the King, skip it and go straight for
  the King instead. For structures built right at the King's doorstep.

> **Forward-looking note:** all King/structure distance math runs through
> one shared helper, `MonsterAI.DistanceBetween(a, b)` — straight-line
> today. When real pathfinding lands (Phase 4), swap that one function for
> real path length and the King-progress guard above automatically starts
> respecting broken walls/new shortest routes, with no other changes
> needed. See `ROADMAP.md`'s Phase 4 section.

### Checklist
- [ ] Prioritizes Structures checkbox is gone; range fields alone control it
- [ ] Structure Interest Range replaces the old ratio fields
- [ ] A monster only detours to a structure that's actually closer to the King than it is
- [ ] Structure Near King Range skips structures too close to the King

## From Guide 13 — Catapult impact mark

A basic placeholder visual showing where a splash shot actually landed — a
circle sized to match Splash Radius, so you can see exactly how far the
Catapult's blast reached. Only appears on splash hits (the Archer's
single-target arrow doesn't need one).

On the **`CatapultStone`** prefab's **Projectile** component, under **Impact
mark [Placeholder] — optional**:

- **Impact Mark Sprite** ← the `Circle` sprite from `Assets/Sprites`.
- **Impact Mark Color** — defaults to a muted brown-gray placeholder.
- **Impact Mark Diameter** (tiles, `0` = auto) — leave at `0` to
  automatically match the real blast area (2 × Splash Radius). Set a
  specific number instead if you want the visual bigger/smaller than the
  actual damage radius. (Added after the initial version, which had no
  independent size control — everything sizeable in this project should be
  an editable field, so this was added to fix that gap.)
- **Impact Mark Seconds** — how long it stays visible, default `0.4`.
- **Impact Mark Sorting Order** — default `4`, a ground marker beneath
  structures/characters.

Leave `Arrow`'s own Impact Mark Sprite empty — the Archer has no splash, so
nothing would show there anyway (gated on Splash Radius > 0). **Note:** the
Catapult Tower's **Projectile Prefab** field has to actually point at
`CatapultStone`, not `Arrow` — if it's set to Arrow, splash damage is riding
on the Arrow's own (unwired) fields and no mark will ever show, since Arrow
was never given an Impact Mark Sprite.

### Checklist
- [ ] CatapultStone's Impact Mark Sprite wired to Circle
- [ ] Catapult Tower's Projectile Prefab is set to CatapultStone (not Arrow)
- [ ] A circle appears at the Catapult's landing point, sized to Splash Radius
- [ ] Setting Impact Mark Diameter to a specific value overrides that auto-sizing
- [ ] Archer's single-target arrow shows no mark (Splash Radius is 0)

## Tower DPS readout (automatic)

Same convenience as the monster definitions' DPS box, now for towers. Click
any tower prefab (Archer Tower, Pike Tower, Catapult — anything using the
shared **Attack Tower** component) and a blue info box appears at the top of
its Inspector: **Estimated DPS (per target hit)**, computed from Damage ÷
Seconds Between Shots. Updates live as you edit the fields below it.

For a **Splash Radius > 0** tower (the Catapult), the box adds a note that
Splash Radius doesn't raise this number — the same Damage lands on
*everything* caught in the blast, it just means more targets share that DPS
at once, not a bigger hit on any one of them. For a tower with no Projectile
Prefab (the Pike, instant melee), it notes the same DPS cycle still applies
even without a projectile in flight.

Praise the King Tower doesn't get this box — it doesn't use Attack Tower and
deals no damage, so DPS doesn't apply to it.

### Checklist
- [ ] Selecting Archer Tower (or any Attack-Tower-based prefab) shows the Estimated DPS box at the top of its Inspector
- [ ] The number updates live as Damage / Seconds Between Shots change
- [ ] Catapult's box notes that Splash Radius spreads the same damage to more targets, not more damage per target
